# Before you start

You need only four things. Claude handles everything else during setup,
including creating your accounts, one step at a time.

- [ ] **The Claude desktop app on a paid plan** (Pro or Max).
      Download it from <https://claude.ai/download>, sign in, and check that you can
      see the **Code** tab. Upgrade at <https://claude.ai/upgrade> if needed.
      *Windows only:* if Claude asks you to install "Git for Windows", accept –
      it's a one-time install.
- [ ] **A bank card.** Setup creates three accounts in your name, and each one
      asks for a card:
  - **Cloudflare** hosts the app and keeps the recordings. It's free, but needs a card to switch storage on.
  - **OpenAI** provides the speaking voice and turns speech into text. Add US$5–10 credit.
  - **Anthropic** provides Claude, the gentle interviewer. Add US$5–10 credit.
- [ ] **Your email inbox**, open nearby, for the sign-up verification emails.
- [ ] **About an hour** with your computer, most of it spent talking with Claude
      about your parent and shaping their questions.

It also helps to think beforehand about what the app should call your parent,
the big chapters of their life, and anything to steer away from. And keep your
phone handy to try the link before you send it.

## Start

1. Download the kit (the link you were sent) and unzip it into your Documents folder.
2. Open the Claude desktop app → **Code** tab → choose the unzipped folder.
3. Type `/setup` and press Enter.

You can stop at any point. Next time, open the same folder and say **continue setup**.

## Want to save time? (optional)

The slowest part is usually opening the two AI accounts, because they may ask
you to verify your phone number. You can do these ahead of time:
- [ ] OpenAI: sign up at <https://platform.openai.com/signup> and add credit under
      **Settings → Billing**. This is separate from a ChatGPT subscription.
- [ ] Anthropic: sign up at <https://console.anthropic.com> and add credit under
      **Settings → Billing**. This is separate from your Claude plan.

Don't create any keys yet – setup tells you when.

## Cost and privacy

- Cloudflare: free. OpenAI and Anthropic: roughly US$1–3 a month each while
  your parent is recording.
- Everything – recordings, transcripts, keys – lives in **your** accounts. Nobody
  who shared this kit with you can see any of it.
